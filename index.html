Off the clock

Happiest on a trail, a bike, or two feet from a net. Most weekends I'm somewhere outside Bengaluru, halfway up a climb, insisting the next ridge is the last one. It never is.

When I'm not trekking I'm on my bike, taking the long route for no particular reason. The rest of my free time goes to comics — reading far too many of them — and then drawing my own. Illustration is the part of design I do purely for myself, where nothing has to convert and no one reviews it.

I'm also annoyingly competitive about badminton and table tennis. If there's a court or a table nearby, I'll find it, and I'll want to keep playing well past when everyone else is done.
