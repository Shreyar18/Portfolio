First, a little context

About FYERS

FYERS is an Indian discount broker offering equity, derivatives and mutual-fund investing through its web and mobile platforms.

Overview

Opening a broking account means KYC, and KYC means documents, cameras and regulators. The flow worked, but people were abandoning it halfway. I mapped where they left, then rebuilt the journey around those moments.

My role

I owned this end to end: the drop-off analysis, the flow, the design and the handoff.
